Visual Art, Paper-III (Essay Type) - Exam Code J-7910

---------------------

SECTION – I (Essay Questions, 20 marks each)

1. (a) Critically explain that Expressionism in European Art offered utmost freedom to artist to represent their anguish and social concern.
   OR
   (b) Compare transformation of image and object between Surrealist artist and Pop artists with examples from sculptural art.
   OR
   (c) Discuss current trends in contemporary Indian Art.
   OR
   (d) Discuss the organization of art and design, in an advertising agency.
   OR
   (e) Mix media played important role in contemporary print making. Explain your views with example.

2. (a) Present a detailed account of prehistoric painting of India.
   OR
   (b) Explain, when classical values were introduced in Indian sculpture and reached the culmination.
   OR
   (c) Discuss the traditional aesthetic theories with reference to Plato and Aristotle.
   OR
   (d) What are the four major printing processes? Explain one of them in detail.
   OR
   (e) Discuss the non-conventional material used in contemporary etching with reference to Jyoti Bhatt.

SECTION – II (Elective Questions, 15 marks each)

Drawing & Painting:
1. Discuss the Colour Field Painting.
2. Discuss the work of Feminist artists Arpana Caur and Gogi Saroj Pal.
3. Describe the peculiar features of Hunting Scenes in Mughal Miniature Painting.

Sculpture:
1. Describe critically the style of Sung Sculpture.
2. Discuss the contribution of Meera Mukherjee in the field of Modern Indian Sculpture.
3. Evaluate the aquatic form of Dada Sculptor Hans Jean Arp.

History of Art:
1. Evaluate the contribution of Anand K. Coomaraswamy to the Indian Art.
2. Describe the architecture of Sun Temple of Konark.
3. Discuss the importance of theory of Rasa in context of Indian Art.

Print Making:
3. Write on Modern American Prints and its impact on art scene in India.
4. Justify the enormous contribution of Bharat Bhawan, Bhopal towards popularizing the discipline of print making in India.
5. Evaluate the contribution of Laxma Gaud in print making.

Applied Art:
3. Estimate the future of advertising and designing in Indian Electronic Media.
4. Discuss the role of digital photography in advertising and designing.
5. Write the conceptual difference amidst advertising, publicity and propaganda.
