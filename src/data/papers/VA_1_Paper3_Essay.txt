Visual Art, Paper-III (Essay Type, 19 questions) - Exam Code D-79-10

---------------------

SECTION – I (Essay Questions, 20 marks each)

1. (a) Trace the evolution of Cubism in European painting.
   OR
   (b) How do you assess the significance of Rodin and Henry Moore as landmarks in modern sculpture?
   OR
   (c) Critically evaluate the achievements of the Renaissance Art with reference to great masters.
   OR
   (d) What are the major principles of Graphic Design and how do they help in creating design? Explain with suitable examples.
   OR
   (e) Explain the prints of Krishna Reddy with his method of viscosity print making process.

2. (a) Discuss the development of Deccani School of Painting.
   (b) Critically assess that Classicism of Gupta Art.
   (c) Which phase of Indian Sculpture is considered as formative stage of Indian idiom? Explain with appropriate examples.
   (d) Describe the design of commercial advertising campaign.
   (e) Write an essay on Francesco de Goya's contribution towards the art of aquatint against the socio-political milieu.

SECTION – II (Elective Questions, 15 marks each)

Drawing & Painting:
1. What is Fauvism? Describe its chief characteristic features with reference to Matisse.
2. Compare the characteristic features of Folk and Classical Art.
3. Discuss the abstract elements in the art of J. Swaminathan.

Sculpture:
3. Describe the Chola Bronzes with special reference to the Nataraj image.
4. Critically assess the work of Ramkinkar Baij with reference to Santhal family.
5. Describe the contribution of Brancusi to the modern Sculpture.

History of Art:
3. Define the word Baroque and discuss its salient features with reference to the sculptures of Gianlorenzo Bernini.
4. Describe the Nagar Style of Temple with special reference to Khajuraho group of Temples.
5. Discuss the Hegelian triad.

Print Making:
3. Write about Carol Summer and his printing methods.
4. Discuss Rembrandt's Dry Point and Etching.
5. What is the contribution of Somenath Hore in print making? Explain his techniques of print making.

Applied Art:
3. Describe different printing Medias and explain any one of them.
4. What is Multimedia presentation? Discuss its role in graphic design.
5. What is Brand image and how does it help in promotion of sale?
