SECTION – I
Note: This section consists of two essay type questions of twenty (20) marks each, to be answered in about five hundred (500) words each. (2 × 20 = 40 Marks)

1. (a) Critically explain the characteristics of Baroque Painting with reference to Peter Paul Rubens.
OR
(b) Discuss with examples the difference between Classical Art of India and the West with reference to their characteristic features.
OR
(c) Discuss the development of Sculpture during 19th and 20th Century Indian Art with special reference to various art movements.
OR
(d) Discuss with thumbnail, the development of Conceptual 'Poster Art' form in Poland. Name a few artists and their revolutionary poster design from Poland, Cuba, Japan and America.
OR
(e) Discuss critically the work of any two leading contemporary Printmakers of India.

2. (a) Discuss the origin and development of Buddhist "Stupa" Architecture.
OR
(b) Compare the stylistic features of 'Pala' and 'Jain' manuscript painting.
OR
(c) Define 'Constructivism'. Write in detail about the leading sculptor of this movement.
OR
(d) Discuss the anatomy of Type faces with reference to their two major board-grouping or basic classifications, though there are millions of typefaces.
OR
(e) Illustrate the latest techniques of Graphic Arts.

SECTION – II
Note: This section contains three (3) questions. From each of the electives/specializations the candidate has to choose only one elective/specialization and answer all the three questions from it. Each question carries fifteen (15) marks and is to be answered in about three hundred (300) words. (3 × 15 = 45 Marks)

Elective – II: Drawing & Painting

2. Comment on Indian Modern painters who have been inspired by the indigenous Art.
3. Evaluate the impact of Art Mart and Global Marketing of Indian Contemporary Art.

Elective – III: Sculpture

4. Mixed Media played an important role in contemporary sculpture. Give an account of your observations.
5. Bronze Casting is an ancient Indian technique of Sculpture. Explain the technique with the help of diagrams.

Elective – IV: Print Making

4. Give an account of Print Biennale held in Bharat Bhawan, Bhopal.

Elective – V: Applied Art
3. Pugmarks or footprint were the 'signs' with which begun the history of Human communication- Discuss.
4. Discuss the five topmost popular campaigns by Indian Ad Agencies.
5. Discuss and differentiate the analogous and digital Animation techniques.
