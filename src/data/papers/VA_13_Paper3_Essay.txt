Visual Art, Paper-III (Essay Type) - Exam Code J-79-11

---------------------

SECTION – I (Essay Questions, 20 marks each)

1. (a) Discuss the architecture of 'Taj Mahal', as the highest achievement of Indo-Islamic architecture.
   OR
   (b) Discuss the major trends of contemporary Indian Painting.
   OR
   (c) Discuss the development of Sculptural Art from the British Art School till the contemporary period.
   OR
   (d) Discuss the Celebrity Endorsement in building of Brand Image of Corporates. Name Five Celebrities as Brand Ambassadors and the products they endorsed.
   OR
   (e) Write a critical note on the Etchings created by Albrecht Durer.

2. (a) Critically examine the merits of Gandharan Art and its impact on Indian Art of Post-Kushana period.
   OR
   (b) Evaluate the art style of Saroj Gogi Pal with reference to feminism.
   OR
   (c) Write in detail about two important Indian Contemporary Ceramic Sculptors with a special reference to their work.
   OR
   (d) What is Offset Printing? Discuss the functions of its different operational units.
   OR
   (e) Discuss the print making technique of contemporary Indian Artist Anupam Sud.
