Visual Art, Paper-III (Essay Type, 26 questions) - Exam Code D-7909

---------------------

SECTION – I (Based on passage, 5 marks each)

Passage about visual language, gallery space vs public space, technology in art, and art's responsible role in society.

1. Explain importance of 'gallery space' and 'public space'.
2. What are the advantages and disadvantages of 'technology' in art production?
3. Describe the role of 'Visual' production in public spaces.
4. Why confrontations take place between artist and society?
5. What is a potential danger involved in politically overtoned images in public spaces?

SECTION – II (Short Answer, 5 marks each)

6. What is Manuscript Paintings? Explain.
7. What do you mean by Ragamala Paintings? Give examples.
8. Appreciate any work of art by applying Shadanga or Six Limbs.
9. What are the advantages of classified advertising on the web?
10. Distinguish the difference between 'readership' and 'circulation'.
11. Define corporate advertising with its various aspects.
12. What is marble? Give geological information of its formation.
13. Write in brief on contemporary mix-media sculptures.
14. Why Ram Kinkar Baiz is considered as 'The Father of Modern Indian Sculptures'?
15. What is the role of roller-press machine in the print-making?
16. How is 'acid-bath' effective in metal plate printing process?
17. Who is Jeram Patel? Write about his prints.
18. Compare Krishna Reddy's print with Haku Shah's print.
19. Who is Balbir Singh Katt? Write about his sculptures.
20. Write a brief note on Gandhara Art.

SECTION – III (Essay Questions, 20 marks each)

21. (a) Discuss the philosophical basis of 'Installation Art' with special reference to any Indian installation artist.
    OR
    (b) Describe the unique features of Mughal Architecture with reference to Fatehpur Sikri, Tomb of Humayun and Jami Masjid.
    OR
    (c) Trace the evolution of Cubism in European painting with suitable examples.
    OR
    (d) What are the major printing processes? Elaborate any one of them.
    OR
    (e) Describe in detail the planning and designing for an advertisement campaign.

22. (a) Evaluate the concept of 'Ready-made' in context of Marcel Duchamp and trace its further development.
    OR
    (b) Describe the evolution of Nagara Style of Temple.
    OR
    (c) Discuss the contribution of Subramanyan and Raza in the development of Modern Indian Art.
    OR
    (d) Explain techniques and aesthetic aspects of 'Viscosity Printing'.
    OR
    (e) What is Corporate Identity? Explain its scope in the present context.

SECTION – IV (Elective Questions, 15 marks each)

Drawing & Painting:
1. Describe the artistic achievement of Amrita Shergil with reference to her important paintings.
2. Critically evaluate the paintings of Rabindranath Tagore.
3. Discuss the art and style of Salvador Dali with reference to his famous paintings.

Sculpture:
1. Discuss Mathura School of sculpture and its Iconography.
2. Critically evaluate the works of Meera Mukherjee.
3. Write about Naum Gabo and his Constructivist Sculpture.

History of Art:
1. Describe the key characteristics of Ajanta Murals.
2. Discuss the concept of Rasa in Indian Aesthetics.
3. Write about the Impressionist Movement and its key artists.

Print Making:
1. Describe the techniques and contribution of Somnath Hore.
2. Discuss the evolution of Woodcut printing.
3. Write about the Japanese Ukiyo-e prints and their influence on European art.

Applied Art:
1. Discuss the role of typography in graphic design.
2. What is Brand Management? Explain with examples.
3. Describe the impact of digital media on advertising design.
